# Plumbing Systems Reference

*Code values below are common IPC/UPC-style figures — the LOCALLY ADOPTED code and AHJ govern. Verify before roughing in.*

## DWV Fundamentals

- Drain slope: **1/4" per foot** for 2-1/2" and smaller; **1/8" per foot allowed for 3" and larger** (too steep is also bad — liquids outrun solids)
- Every trap needs a vent; **trap arm max distance (at 1/4"/ft): 1-1/4" → 5 ft · 1-1/2" → 6 ft · 2" → 8 ft · 3" → 10 ft · 4" → 12 ft**
- No S-traps (they siphon dry); P-trap everywhere; trap seal 2–4"
- Cleanouts: at the base of every stack, at changes of direction >45° beyond limits, and every ~100 ft horizontal (50 ft ≤3")
- Wet venting rules are size/fixture specific — check the code section, don't eyeball it

Horizontal branch and stack sizing comes off total DFU per table — e.g., a 3" horizontal branch at 1/4"/ft carries ~20–48 DFU depending on code/branch interval. Always size off the adopted table.

## Water Supply

- Velocities: keep ≤ **8 ft/s cold, 5 ft/s hot** (erosion + noise)
- Pressure: 40–80 psi ideal; **>80 psi needs a PRV** (code) + expansion tank on closed systems
- Water hammer arrestors at quick-closing valves (ice maker, washer, flush valves, solenoids)
- Hot delivery: recirculation or point-of-use for long runs; insulate hot lines
- Water heater: T&P relief piped full-size, gravity, visible termination — **never capped, never reduced, never threaded at the end**; expansion tank on any closed system (PRV/backflow present)

## Backflow Protection (choose by hazard)

| Device | Protects against | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Air gap | Everything (best) | Sinks, tanks — 2× pipe diameter, ≥1" |
| AVB (atmospheric vacuum breaker) | Backsiphonage only, low hazard | No continuous pressure, no valves downstream; 6" above highest outlet |
| PVB (pressure vacuum breaker) | Backsiphonage, can be under pressure | Irrigation; 12" above highest outlet |
| DC (double check) | Backpressure + siphonage, LOW hazard | Fire lines, non-health hazard |
| **RPZ** | Both, HIGH hazard | Chemical, boiler feed, medical — the only answer for health hazards; needs drain for relief discharge |

Testable devices (PVB/DC/RPZ) get annual certification by a certified tester.

## Gas Piping (basics — always size from the code tables)

- Natural gas: low pressure ~**7" WC (~1/4 psi)** inlet, ~3.5" WC manifold; propane ~11" WC
- Size off the LONGEST-RUN method + total CFH load from the adopted code's tables — never guess a gas line
- Sediment trap/drip leg at each appliance, ahead of controls
- Unions/valves: shutoff within 6 ft of appliance, accessible
- Buried gas: coated/wrapped steel or PE with tracer wire; yellow CSST needs bonding per manufacturer
- Test: air test per code (commonly 1.5× working, min 3 psig for low-pressure, hold 10 min) — never oxygen
- Leak check: soap solution/electronic detector on every joint — **never flame**

## Practical Plumbing Details

- Closet flange on TOP of finished floor, not below it; wax ring or better — shimming a rocking toilet breaks seals
- Dielectric union where copper meets steel; no copper downstream of galvanized (rust plugs)
- Stub-outs: cap and pressure-test BEFORE close-up; photo every wall before drywall
- PVC: primer + cement, quarter-turn hold 30 sec; support horizontal every 4 ft (3" and under); no PVC for hot >140°F (use CPVC)
- Trench bedding for buried pipe; no sharp rock contact; sleeves where passing through foundation
