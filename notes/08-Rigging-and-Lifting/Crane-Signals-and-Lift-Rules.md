# Crane Hand Signals & Lift Rules

*Standard ASME B30.5 signals — learn these until they're reflexes. One signal person; anyone can give STOP.*

## The Core Signals

| Signal | Motion |
|---|---|
| **Hoist (up)** | Arm vertical, index finger up, small circles |
| **Lower** | Arm down, index finger pointing down, small circles |
| **Stop** | Arm extended, palm down, hold rigid |
| **Emergency stop** | Both arms extended, palms down, sweep back and forth |
| **Swing** | Arm extended, point with finger in direction of swing |
| **Boom up** | Arm out, thumb up, fist closed |
| **Boom down** | Arm out, thumb down, fist closed |
| **Boom up + lower load** | Thumb up, other fist opens/closes |
| **Boom down + hoist** | Thumb down, other fist opens/closes |
| **Travel (crawler/mobile)** | Fists in front, rotate around each other in direction |
| **Extend boom** | Fists closed, thumbs out |
| **Retract boom** | Fists closed, thumbs in |
| **Dog everything (pause)** | Hands clasped at waist |
| **Move slowly** | One hand gives the motion signal, other hand held motionless above it |

## Lift Rules That Keep People Alive

- **ONE designated signal person** — but ANYONE can and must signal STOP; the operator obeys a stop from anyone
- No hand signals in the dark/blind — use radios with a dedicated channel, confirmed before the pick
- **Never lift over people.** Barricade the fall zone (load + rigging radius); the operator's blind zone is yours
- Load stays as low as practical during travel; tagline in hand, not wrapped on anything
- Test the rigging: lift a few inches, hold, check balance + brake, then lift
- Never leave a suspended load unattended; never ride the hook/load
- Wind: watch the chart limit and the load's sail area — a light, wide load (ductwork, panels) sails early
- Set-down: landing zone pre-built with cribbing; hands clear of pinch points; rigging off only when stable

## Pre-Lift Checklist (run it every time)

1. Verified weight + CG; rigging capacity checked WITH angles
2. All rigging inspected THIS SHIFT, tags legible
3. Crane: current annual + daily operator inspection; outriggers/mats set; level
4. Chart checked at max radius; wind OK; ground confirmed
5. Path + set-down zone clear; exclusion zone barricaded
6. Signal person designated; radio check done
7. Everyone in the crew knows the plan AND the stop signal
