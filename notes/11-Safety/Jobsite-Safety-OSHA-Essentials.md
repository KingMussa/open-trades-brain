# Jobsite Safety — OSHA Essentials

*The rules that get enforced, the numbers that get cited, and what actually keeps you whole.*

## The Focus Four (what kills construction workers)

1. **Falls** — protection required at **6 ft** (construction): guardrails, PFAS, or covers. Harness: anchor ≥5,000 lb/person, free fall ≤6 ft, know your total fall distance (lanyard + stretch + your height + margin). Inspect harness every use; **any fall = retire the gear**
2. **Struck-by** — hard hats, barricades under overhead work, toe boards/screens, high-vis around equipment, never under suspended loads
3. **Caught-in/between** — trench protection at **5 ft deep** (or 4 ft+ in some soils/requirements); spoil pile ≥**2 ft** back from edge; ladder within **25 ft** of every worker; competent person inspects daily + after rain
4. **Electrocution** — LOTO always; treat every circuit as live until YOU prove it dead (live-dead-live)

## LOTO (the procedure that saves you)

1. Notify affected workers → 2. Identify ALL energy sources (electrical, water pressure, gravity, thermal, stored/spring) → 3. Shut down in order → 4. Isolate every source → 5. **Apply YOUR lock + tag** (one person, one lock — you hold the only key) → 6. Release/block stored energy → 7. **TRY IT** — attempt start, verify zero energy with meter/gauge → 8. Work → 9. Only the person who locked removes the lock
- Group lockout: lockbox with every worker's lock on it — nobody works unprotected

## The Cited Numbers

- **Fall protection trigger:** 6 ft general construction; 15 ft steel erection connectors; 10 ft scaffolds
- **Scaffold:** base ratio 4:1 max height-to-base (outriggers beyond); fully planked; guardrails ≥10 ft; access ladder/stairs — never climb cross-braces; competent person inspects each shift; tag it
- **Ladders:** extend **3 ft above** landing; 4:1 angle; 3-point contact; inspect; never top step
- **Trench/excavation:** protective system ≥5 ft; ladder within 25 ft; competent person on site; no entry to unprotected trench regardless of depth "for a second"
- **Confined space:** permit required when hazards exist — test atmosphere BEFORE and DURING (O2 19.5–23.5%, LEL <10%, toxics), attendant posted, retrieval system, rescue plan (911 is NOT a rescue plan)
- **Hot work:** permit, 35-ft combustible zone, extinguisher, fire watch during + **30–60 min after**
- **Silica:** cut/grind concrete/stone with water or vacuum — the dust is the killer; table 1 controls
- **Electrical approach:** qualified persons only near exposed energized parts; arc-flash PPE per the label/study — READ THE LABEL at each panel

## Heat & Environment

- Water-Rest-Shade: heat index 90°F+ = crew brief; 95°F+ = scheduled breaks; new workers get acclimatization ramp (most heat deaths happen in week one)
- Know heat exhaustion (sweating, weakness, clammy — cool them down) vs **heat stroke (hot, dry OR wet, confused — CALL 911, ice now)**
- Cold: wind chill plan; hydrate anyway; frostbite on fingers first

## Housekeeping & Behavior

- Broom-clean standard daily; cords/hoses out of walkways; materials stacked stable
- Struck-by basics: never position between a load and an immovable object; pinch-point awareness on every set
- Report every near miss — it's a free lesson; the next one charges tuition
- **Stop Work Authority is real:** anyone stops any unsafe work, no retaliation. If your gut says no, it's no

## OSHA Recordables (what GF/foreman paperwork means)

- Recordable: days away, restricted duty, medical treatment beyond first aid, loss of consciousness
- Report to OSHA: fatality within **8 hours**; in-patient hospitalization/amputation/eye loss within **24 hours**
