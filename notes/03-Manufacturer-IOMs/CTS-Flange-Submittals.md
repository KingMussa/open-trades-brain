*Sources: ctsflange.com product pages + PDFs. All: solder joint to ASME B16.22, drilling ANSI B16.5.*

## CC series — Copper Coated Flange 150lb

- Service temp **-66°F to +400°F** · Working pressure **450 PSI** · Plate steel
- Backing flange ASTM B16.5, drilled ANSI B16.5
- **No EPDM insulator required** — copper-on-copper contact eliminates electrolysis risk. 100% lead free
- Sizes **1-1/2" thru 6"** · For harsh environments, in-ground or aggressive atmosphere; water, gas, steam above/below ground

## SF series — Copper Press Fit Flange 150lb (formerly Spigot)

- Service temp **-66°F to +272°F** · Working **450 PSI**; **burst 1000 PSI (2–4")** · ANSI B16.5 drilling
- Sizes **2" thru 4"**: SF002 (2", .63) · SF025 (2-1/2", .63) · SF003 (3", .63) · SF004 (4", .709)
- Usable on any press-fit system, soldering, or grooving; all brass companion flange applications
- No cracking/warping from overheating — heating/cooling time reduced or eliminated
- **Adjustable rotating flange eliminates two-holing**; 100% electrolysis protection, no dielectric insulator cost; 99.9% copper (lead free)
- Applications: butterfly + flanged gate valve installs, pumps, boilers, chillers, backflow devices
- Available 150lb and 300lb

PDF: https://www.ctsflange.com/wp-content/resources/Copper_Press_FitSpigot_150lb.pdf
