# Welding, Brazing & Soldering

*WPS/PQR and project spec always govern. This is the field-level "why and how" behind them.*

## The Three Processes — Know the Line

- **Soldering:** filler melts **below 840°F** — copper domestic/hydronic small lines, low pressure
- **Brazing:** filler melts **above 840°F** but below the base metal — high-strength copper joints, refrigerant (ACR), high-pressure hydronic
- **Welding:** base metals actually fuse together — steel pipe, structural

## Soldering Copper (95/5 & lead-free)

- Lead-free solder REQUIRED for potable (no exceptions since 1986/2014 rules)
- Clean to bright metal (sand cloth — never steel wool), flux thin on BOTH surfaces, assemble while wet
- Heat the FITTING (it's the heat sink), feed solder when a touch melts — capillary action pulls it in; a fillet all around = done
- Wipe the joint while warm; don't move it until solid
- Overheating burns flux → leak. If it didn't take, cool, re-clean, redo — don't pile solder on a dirty joint

## Brazing Copper (BCuP / silver)

- Joint clearance 0.002–0.005" per side (per brazing spec); too tight = filler won't flow, too loose = weak
- Copper-to-copper with BCuP (15% silver class): **self-fluxing, no flux** · copper-to-brass/bronze or steel: **flux required**
- **ACR/refrigerant ALWAYS with nitrogen purge** (typical spec: 99.998% N2, ≥1 min pre-purge, continuous until cool, 1/8" restrictor ≥8" from joint) — no purge = copper oxide flakes = destroyed TXVs/compressors later
- Neutral flame; heat evenly; feed filler at the far side of the joint — let capillary pull it THROUGH (proves full penetration)
- **NEVER quench a braze joint** — cool naturally
- Stay-Silv 15 (15% Ag) = general copper; 45% Ag (BAg) = dissimilar metals, better flow; check spec

## SMAW (Stick) Pipe Basics

- **E6010/E6011**: deep-digging root pass, whipping motion, the standard pipe root
- **E7018**: low-hydrogen fill and cap — keep rods DRY (rod oven); smooth, strong, less spatter
- Root pass is everything: consistent keyhole, tie-ins at the top (12 o'clock), grind starts/stops
- Typical WPS: 6010 root + 7018 hot/fill/cap on carbon steel — YOUR WPS governs, not habit
- Preheat/interpass per WPS for thick wall or high carbon; don't weld on wet/frozen pipe
- Weld defects: undercut (too hot/fast), porosity (contamination/short arc), lack of fusion (cold, bad angle), slag inclusions (incomplete cleaning between passes)

## GTAW (TIG)

- The quality process: stainless, thin wall, critical roots
- **Stainless root needs an argon BACK-PURGE** (~5–10 cfh until capped) — unpurged stainless root = sugared, weak, fails inspection
- Clean filler, clean gloves — contamination is the enemy

## Oxy-Acetylene Cutting/Heating

- Neutral flame: sharp blue cone; carburizing (excess acetylene, feather) = sooty; oxidizing = hissing short cone (for cutting only)
- Check valves + flashback arrestors on BOTH torch and regulators; crack cylinder valves before attaching regulators; acetylene never above **15 psig**
- Cylinders chained upright, caps on when moving, O2 and fuel separated in storage (20 ft or 5 ft wall)

## Hot Work Controls

- Permit BEFORE first spark, every day, each area
- 35-ft combustible check: remove/protect with fire blanket; sparks fall and roll — check floors BELOW
- Fire watch during work + **30–60 minutes after** (per permit); extinguisher charged and at hand
- Welding screens for bystanders; fume control on galv (metal fume fever — galvanized needs respirator/ventilation)
