# General Foreman — Running the Whole Job

*From running a crew to running the site: what changes, what the GC expects, and how jobs are won or lost.*

## What Changes at GF

- You run **through your foremen**, not around them. Give them the plan, the priorities, the resources — then hold them accountable for their crews. Jumping past them to direct their men destroys them
- Your product is **flow**: material, information, and access arriving ahead of the work. A journeyman waits because a GF failed three days ago
- You own the **coordination space** between trades: who's in the rack first, who's above whom, whose penetration is whose

## The GF Week

- **Mon:** verify last week's quantities vs plan with each foreman; update % complete; manpower moves for the week
- **Tue:** GC coordination meeting — bring your 3-week lookahead, your blockers with owners/dates, and your RFI/change status. **Never attend without written notes**
- **Wed:** walk every active area yourself (2 hrs minimum). Your eyes, not reports
- **Thu:** procurement check — every long-lead item vs need date; escalate anything slipping
- **Fri:** safety walk + quality sampling; next week's plan issued to foremen in writing

## Schedule Mechanics

- Know the **critical path** on YOUR scope. Float belongs to the project — spend it deliberately, never accidentally
- 3-week lookahead feeds the GC's 6-week. Garbage in = GC stops trusting your dates
- Recovery, in order of preference: **resequence → prefab → add crew to critical path → extend hours → add shift**. Overtime is the most expensive answer and the first one amateurs reach for
- **Prefab is the cheat code**: anything built in a shop costs a fraction of field hours — spools, racks, valve assemblies, headers. Push prefab early; by the time you need it, it's too late to start

## Money: Changes, T&M, Backcharges

- **Extra work rule:** No extra work without direction IN WRITING (CCD, RFI response, or email). Verbal direction = you work for free
- **T&M tickets:** same-day, signed by GC rep daily — hours by name, material itemized, equipment listed. A ticket signed Friday for Monday's work is fiction and everyone knows it
- Track changed work on a separate code. Weekly: submit costs before memories fade
- Notice provisions: most contracts want written notice in X days of a change event. Miss it = own it
- Backcharge defense = your daily reports + photos. That's why they're non-negotiable

## Information Discipline

- **RFI:** write it so it can't be dodged — specific question, specific location, "we propose X" attached. Log date sent; a late answer is a documented delay
- Current drawings on site ALWAYS. Superseded prints pulled or stamped VOID. The costliest rework in construction is an old drawing
- Submittals before procurement, procurement before install. Installing unapproved material can make it YOURS at your cost

## People at Scale

- Foremen meeting weekly: 30 min, standing, three questions — what got done, what's next, what's in the way
- Headcount plan: know your ramp curve (start small, peak mid-job, taper). Carrying peak manpower past peak burns the job's profit in weeks
- Layoffs: cut by performance and versatility, never by convenience; keep your core
- The GC's super, the inspector, the owner's rep: manage UP with early bad news. Bad news early is a problem shared; bad news late is your fault

## Quality & Closeout (where jobs actually end)

- Punch as you go — **an area is done when it's punched**, not when the last weld cools
- Start closeout documents at 50%: as-built markups weekly, O&Ms collected as equipment arrives, test reports filed when signed
- Turnover package: as-builts, O&M manuals, warranties, test reports, training records, spare parts + attic stock, valve schedules, keys/passes returned
- Final walk: bring nothing to fix. Pre-walk it three days before with your foremen and fix everything first

## The GF Mindset

- **Safety is schedule** — an accident stops more work than rain ever will
- Own the weather plan, the holiday plan, and the delivery cutoffs around both
- Your reputation = jobs finished clean, men who'd work for you again, GCs who ask for you. All three are built one honest week at a time
